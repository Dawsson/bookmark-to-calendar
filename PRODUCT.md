# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who bookmark posts on X because they intend to revisit them, usually while browsing fast and context-switching. They need a small interruption that turns a saved post into an actual review moment.

## Product Purpose

Bookmark to Calendar opens a focused reminder prompt after a user bookmarks an X post. Success means the user can schedule a review without thinking, and the extension stays out of the way otherwise.

## Brand Personality

Direct, clean, useful. The product should feel like a sharp browser utility, not a motivational app or a marketing page.

## Anti-references

No AI-slop gradients, fake rounded app-icon corners, verbose onboarding, bloated copy, decorative cards, or dramatic productivity-culture language.

## Design Principles

- The prompt should feel immediate and obvious.
- Every word should help the user decide what to do next.
- Alignment and sizing should be exact because the UI appears over someone else’s product.
- The icon should be simple, filled, and legible at extension-toolbar scale.
- Calendar handoff should remain explicit, never silently saving events.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, visible focus states, semantic dialog structure, keyboard dismissal with Escape, and reduced-motion-safe interactions.
